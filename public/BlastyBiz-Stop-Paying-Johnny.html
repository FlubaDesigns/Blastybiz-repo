What Does Johnny Actually Do All Week?

Here's the honest thing about the job Johnny's doing for you: most of it is writing the same content in eight slightly different ways, reformatting it for each platform, logging in and out of your accounts, and posting it one at a time.

Google wants local keywords and a clear call-to-action. Instagram wants something punchy with a couple of hashtags. Facebook wants a warmer, more conversational tone. Craigslist wants plain text with no fluff. Each one is different, each one takes time to do right, and each one needs to happen every single week.

That's exactly what BlastyBiz does. You fill out one form — your business info, your offer, whatever's relevant this week — and AI rewrites it correctly for every platform automatically. You tap publish, or copy-paste where the platform doesn't allow direct posting. It takes about two minutes.

So What About Johnny?

Look — we're not saying fire Johnny. Johnny's a good kid, working through school or saving up for whatever Johnny's saving up for. He's your family (or close enough). You don't want that conversation.

But here's the honest question: if the actual job is "keep our business showing up everywhere people are looking," $228 a year handles most of it. Reliably. Every week. Without anyone having to remember.

What do you actually need Johnny for after that? Maybe nothing changes. Maybe there's something more valuable he can do with those hours for you — something AI genuinely can't. Or maybe you have the most polite conversation of the year and everyone comes out ahead. Spending a lot more time at the beach! Or studying, we meant!

Either way, you should at least know the number before you decide.
