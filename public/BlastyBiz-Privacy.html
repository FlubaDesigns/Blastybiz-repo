Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Fluba Designs LLC

What This Is

BlastyBiz is a marketing distribution tool for businesses of all kinds, built by Fluba Designs LLC. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. By using BlastyBiz, you agree to this policy.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We collect only what is needed to run the service.

Information We Collect

Account information: When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and authentication method (email/password or a connected sign-in provider).

Business profile: Business name, owner name, phone number, email, address, website, hours of operation, business category, and tone preference.

Listing content: The offer descriptions, pricing, and photos you upload to create listings. This content is stored so AI can adapt it for each platform.

Platform connections: Which platforms you have enabled or connected, and connection status. OAuth tokens for connected platforms are stored encrypted.

Usage data: Listing history, publish job status, and activity logs.

Payment information: Billing is handled entirely by Square. BlastyBiz does not store credit card numbers. We store payment-provider customer and subscription identifiers, subscription status, plan, billing period, and billing amounts needed to manage your account.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and adapt your business listings using AI
  • To post listings to connected platforms on your behalf
  • To display your listing history and publishing status
  • To manage your subscription and enforce plan limits
  • To send account, onboarding, publishing, billing, and retention notices
  • To improve the service and fix bugs

We do not use your business content or listing data to train AI models.

AI Processing

Text features use Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude, depending on the configured feature and provider. Requests can include your business details, website-derived context, saved business and campaign memory, listing text, and messages you enter.

When you request an AI image, Google Gemini receives your image description and approved post context. The generated image is saved with your campaign.

These providers process requests under their own policies: Google and Anthropic. BlastyBiz does not send uploaded photo files to these AI features.

Google API Data

When you connect Google Business Profile, BlastyBiz requests the business.manage OAuth scope. This scope supports selecting your business location, importing its profile details and photos, and publishing posts you authorize.

  • BlastyBiz reads your location list and selected business profile details and photos to set up your business and choose where to post.
  • BlastyBiz creates posts on your behalf using AI-adapted content you have reviewed and approved.
  • Your Google OAuth access token and refresh token are stored encrypted in our database. They are never sold, shared with third parties, or used outside the business profile features you authorize.
  • Google sign-in can supply your name, email, and profile image for your account. Business Profile access does not grant access to your Gmail, Drive, or contacts.
  • You can revoke BlastyBiz's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting from within BlastyBiz also removes your stored tokens immediately.

BlastyBiz's use of Google API data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate BlastyBiz:

  • Firebase / Google Cloud — Authentication, database, file storage, and hosting.
  • Google Gemini and Anthropic — AI text processing; Gemini also generates requested images.
  • Resend — Delivery of account and service emails, including recipient addresses and email content.
  • Square — Payment processing and subscription management.
  • Google Business Profile API — Auto-posting to Google Business Profile (when authorized by you). See Google API Data section above.
  • Meta Graph API — Auto-posting to Facebook and Instagram (when authorized by you).

Each service operates under its own privacy policy. We share only the minimum data required for each to function.

Photos and Images

Uploaded and generated images are stored in Firebase Storage and associated with your account or campaign. When you publish a post, the selected image is made available to that platform and its audience. Image download links can also be accessed by anyone who has the link. We do not sell your photos.

Data Retention

Paid accounts. Your data is kept as long as your account is active. If you cancel your subscription, your business profile and listing history are retained so you can reactivate later — cancelling removes paid features, not your data.

Free accounts. If a free account goes unused for 12 months, we delete it. "Unused" means nobody has signed in or done anything in the app during that time — simply visiting our public website does not count.

Before anything is deleted we email you at least 14 days beforehand, with a second reminder shortly before the deletion date. Signing in once cancels the deletion and resets the clock. Upgrading to a paid plan also cancels it.

When a free account is deleted we remove your account record, business profile, campaigns, saved listings, drafts, uploaded photos, publishing history and stored platform connections, and we revoke any access tokens you had granted us. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

You can see when your account was last active, and how long we will keep it, on your Account page.

To permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time, use the delete option on your Account page or email info@blastybiz.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Deleted information can remain in restricted recovery backups until those backups expire. Export backups are scheduled for removal after 35 days; point-in-time recovery can retain earlier database versions for up to 7 days. Temporary restore-test databases are checked for removal after 7 days when a recovery drill runs. We retain a minimal deletion record so deleted accounts are removed again before restored data is returned to service.

Your Rights

You can request a copy of your data, correct inaccurate information, or request deletion at any time by contacting us at info@blastybiz.com.

If you are in the European Union or California, you have additional rights under GDPR and CCPA respectively. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

BlastyBiz uses Firebase Authentication and browser storage to keep you signed in and remember app preferences. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party analytics platform.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date above and notify active users by email. Continued use of BlastyBiz after changes means you accept the updated policy.
