Create your Page first

It takes about 5 minutes. Once it's live, come back here to connect.

1
Go to facebook.com/pages/create and sign in with your Facebook account.
2
Choose Business or Brand → enter your business name and category.
3
Add a profile photo and cover image (your logo works great). Click Save.
4
Come back here and click Connect on Facebook — BlastyBiz will find your new Page automatically.
