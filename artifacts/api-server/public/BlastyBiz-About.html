Why We Built This

The Problem We Solved

Business owners work harder than anyone. But when it comes to getting found online, they're expected to become social media managers on top of everything else — the plumber who answers on weekends, the salon that knows every customer by name, the restaurant that's been in the same spot for 20 years.

BlastyBiz was built to solve one specific problem: you should be able to describe your business once and have it show up everywhere that matters. No rewriting. No logging into a dozen platforms. No guessing what to say on Instagram vs. Craigslist vs. Google.

One form. AI does the rest. That's the whole idea.
