BlastyBiz was built because local business owners work too hard to spend their evenings copy-pasting listings into 12 different tabs.

Our Story

Why We Built This

Local businesses are the backbone of every community — the plumber who answers on weekends, the salon that knows every customer by name, the restaurant that's been on the same corner for 20 years. They work harder than anyone. But when it comes to getting found online, they're expected to become social media managers on top of everything else.

BlastyBiz was born in Englewood, Florida to solve one specific problem: you should be able to describe your business once and have it show up everywhere that matters. No rewriting. No logging into a dozen platforms. No guessing what to say on Instagram vs. Craigslist vs. Google.

One form. AI does the rest. That's the whole idea.
