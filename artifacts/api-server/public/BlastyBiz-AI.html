The AI That Reads Before It Writes

Most AI tools start from a blank page every time. BlastyBiz doesn't. Before writing a single word, the AI reads everything it knows about your business, your current campaign, and the specific platform it's writing for. That's why the copy sounds like you — not like a template.

Five Layers. Every Generation.

When you hit generate, the AI doesn't guess. It reads five distinct sources of information before writing anything — then writes a separate, adapted version for each platform. Here's what it reads, in order:

  • Your Business Profile — the foundation: what you do, where you are, your hours, your tone
  • Your Business Library — uploaded documents the AI reads every time (menus, flyers, service sheets)
  • Business Memory — permanent facts you've added that are always true about your business
  • Campaign Memory — facts specific to this campaign: the offer, the audience, the angle
  • Platform Rules — format, tone, dos and don'ts specific to each platform being written for

Layer 1 — Your Business Profile

The foundation. Your business name, what you do, where you're located, your hours, your target customers, and your preferred tone. You fill this in once during setup. The AI reads it on every single generation, for every campaign, forever.

This is what keeps the basics right — the AI always knows it's writing for a plumber in Fort Myers, not a bakery in Chicago.

Layer 2 — Your Business Library

Upload any document that describes your business and the AI will read it before writing. A menu. A service list. A pricing sheet. A marketing flyer. A capabilities document.

The AI extracts the text and holds it in your Business Library. Every time you generate copy — for any campaign — it reads the library first. You never have to repeat yourself. Upload once, the AI knows it forever.

Examples of what to upload:

  • Your full service menu with descriptions and pricing
  • A marketing one-pager that describes what makes you different
  • A capabilities document or bio you use for proposals
  • A seasonal promotion sheet with current offers

Layer 3 — Business Memory

Business Memory is your permanent facts layer. It holds everything that's always true about your business — the details that make generic AI copy sound wrong when they're missing.

You add facts in plain English from inside the app — no forms, no fields. Type what you want the AI to know and hit submit. It takes effect on the next generation.

Examples:

  • We've been family-owned since 1987
  • We never use subcontractors — every job is our own crew
  • Same-day service is always available, no extra charge
  • We only work commercial accounts, not residential

Layer 4 — Campaign Memory

Campaign Memory sits on top of everything else. It's specific to what you're running right now — this promotion, this audience, this moment. Each campaign has its own memory. Switch campaigns and the AI's context switches with it.

Examples:

  • 20% off all appointments booked before Friday
  • We're targeting new homeowners this month specifically
  • Emphasize speed — our main competitor is backed up right now
  • Don't mention the winter rate — this is summer pricing

Layer 5 — Platform Rules

Every platform has its own format, tone, and rules. Instagram captions can't have clickable links. Craigslist readers hate emoji. Google Business won't surface descriptions that sound like ads. Nextdoor responds to neighbors, not brands.

BlastyBiz has a full rule set for every platform it writes for — character limits, format requirements, what works, what to avoid, and image specifications. The AI reads the rule set for each platform before writing that platform's copy.

This is why the Instagram caption reads like Instagram and the Craigslist post reads like Craigslist — even though both came from the same campaign in one click.

What Happens When You Generate

When you hit generate, the AI runs through every layer — in order — then writes separately for each active platform:

  • Reads your Business Profile
  • Reads your Business Library documents
  • Reads your Business Memory facts
  • Reads your Campaign Memory facts
  • For each platform: reads that platform's rules, then writes
  • Logs every output to your campaign history

The more layers you've filled in, the sharper the output. A business with a full library, 10 business facts, and a detailed campaign will get dramatically better copy than one that filled in the basics and nothing else.
