The AI That Reads Before It Writes

Most AI tools start from scratch every time. BlastyBiz doesn't. Before writing a single word, the AI reads everything it knows about your business and your campaign. That's why the copy sounds like you — not like a template.

Two Layers of Memory

BlastyBiz keeps two separate pools of information that the AI reads on every generation. You build them up over time. The AI draws on both, every time.

  • Business Memory — always true, always on, applies to every campaign
  • Campaign Memory — specific to this promotion, this audience, this moment

Business Memory — Always On

Business Memory is the permanent layer. It holds everything that's true about your business no matter what you're promoting — your story, your tone, the things that make you different, the details that make generic AI copy sound wrong.

You fill it in once. The AI reads it every single time, for every campaign, forever. Add a new fact anytime — it takes effect on the next generation.

Examples of what goes here:

  • We've been family-owned since 1987
  • We never use subcontractors
  • Same-day service is always available
  • We specialize in commercial accounts, not residential

Campaign Memory — This Moment

Campaign Memory is specific to what you're running right now. A summer promotion. A new service. A price change. A target audience you want to speak to directly. It sits on top of your Business Memory — the AI uses both together.

Each campaign has its own memory. Switch campaigns and the AI's context switches with it. Add new facts to a campaign any time during the run.

Examples of what goes here:

  • 20% off all appointments booked before Friday
  • We're targeting new homeowners this month
  • Emphasize speed — our main competitor is slow right now
  • Don't mention the winter pricing — this is the summer rate

What Happens When You Generate

When you hit generate, the AI runs in this exact order:

  • Reads your Business Memory — the permanent layer
  • Reads your Campaign Memory — the current layer
  • Reads your platform settings — which platforms are active
  • Writes a separate, adapted version for each platform — different format, different tone, same facts
  • Logs the output to your campaign history so you can see every version ever generated

The copy for Instagram won't read like the copy for Craigslist. But both will sound like your business, because both are built from the same memory.

Why This Matters

Generic AI produces generic copy. It doesn't know you opened at 6am. It doesn't know you guarantee your work in writing. It doesn't know you're running a discount this week or that you're trying to reach a specific audience. It just writes.

BlastyBiz AI writes with context. The more you feed the memory, the sharper the output. Over time, the AI builds a real picture of your business — and every piece of copy it produces reflects that.
